Settings

Hold a key, speak, and type into any field. Whisper runs inside the extension. Audio is not sent to a server.

Privacy

Microphone audio is processed on this device. It is not uploaded, not stored on a server, and not used to train a model. There is no account.

  • Default model is bundled. Offline after install.
  • Dictation history stays in Chrome on this profile.
  • Turn the extension off per site from the popup.

Listen mode

Hold keeps recording until you release. Toggle starts and stops on the same key.

Language

Speech model

Tiny English ships inside the extension and never needs the network. Other models ask permission to download once from Hugging Face.

Spoken language

English-only models ignore this. Multilingual models need a match.
